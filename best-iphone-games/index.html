Four free games. Choose the one that suits your mood.

Arcade mischief + logic puzzles

Shut Up Fly

Hold steady. Let it land. Squish. Clear every fly and maggot before the thirty-second campaign clock runs out, earn outrageous weapons and restore an island across 51 levels.

Prefer a puzzle? Flyduko has 300 fly-themed logic boards. Newly earned weapon prizes go back into the arcade hunt.

Try this when you want a quick reaction challenge or a logic puzzle with a comic payoff.

iPhone & iPad · iOS/iPadOS 18+
Free · Optional rewarded ads · No in-app purchases

A relaxed observation puzzle

Bill Sergeant

Inspect both sides of illustrated bills. Genuine notes belong in the money bag; counterfeits go in the trash. Check a familiar face against a suspicious reverse, then review the reasons behind your choices.

There is no ticking clock. A small detail can change the whole decision.

Try this when you want to look closely and make deliberate choices at your own pace.

iPhone & iPad · iOS/iPadOS 17+
Free download · Fictional and altered currency appears in the artwork

Downhill mountain biking

Whoopdy Doo

Pump through rollers, pick a jump line and move your rider to recover a landing. Ride Fern & Fable, Loose Goose and Cloud Nine, chase medals, or explore the mountain in Free Ride.

Best clean lines and progress stay on your device. Play in landscape on your iPhone or iPad.

Try this when you want to learn a line, land a jump and take another run.

iPhone & iPad · iOS/iPadOS 17+

Quiet counted needlework

Nocturne Loom

Choose an illustrated tapestry and stitch it one cross at a time. Six ordinary designs and five canvas sizes let you pick a small sampler or a longer project.

Sleep Weave has six separate dream-themed designs, gentle sound and a cloth that gradually darkens as you stitch.

Try this when you want a quiet creative activity with no score or time pressure.

iPhone & iPad · iOS/iPadOS 17+
Free · No account, ads or in-app purchases · Play offline

03 / Make it a good fit

How to choose the best iPhone game for you.

Start with the kind of attention you want to give.

A reaction game asks you to respond quickly. An observation puzzle gives you time to inspect a detail. A riding game rewards learning a line and adjusting your timing. Needlework offers a longer project you can return to. Pick the activity you want today.

Check the practical details.

Look at the minimum software version, screen orientation and download model. “Free” can describe different experiences: Shut Up Fly offers optional rewarded ads, while Nocturne Loom has no ads or in-app purchases. The App Store listing is the place to check your own device and region.

Choose a first activity, then see if you want another.

Try a campaign room or Flyduko board, inspect a few bills, ride a trail, or begin a small tapestry. Your choice to return tells you more about fit than a broad “best games” label.

A few useful answers.

Are these games available for iPad as well as iPhone?

Yes. All four public App Store releases support iPhone and iPad. Shut Up Fly requires iOS or iPadOS 18 or later; the other three require 17 or later. Whoopdy Doo is designed for landscape play.

Which of these games can I play offline?

Shut Up Fly’s single-player campaign and local records work offline after download; optional rewarded ads and Game Center rankings need internet. Nocturne Loom also supports offline play. Follow each game’s published requirements and check the installed app for optional online features.

What are MUNTED and MUNTED Studio?

MUNTED is the studio wordmark and short name for MUNTED Studio, Tim Hudson’s independent iPhone and iPad game studio. The App Store developer and seller name is Tim Hudson. Meet the studio and its games.
