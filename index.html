Something fun to play on your iPhone

One island. An outrageous infestation.

Shut Up Fly is a free single-player iPhone game with 51 campaign levels. Tap flies before they escape, crush maggots before they hatch, and earn weapons by completing four-hit combos. Chase a better score whenever you have time for another attempt.

The whole campaign is free, with no in-app purchases or Apple Arcade membership required. Play without watching ads, or choose an optional ad to earn one Armoury deployment. The campaign and local records work offline after download; rewarded ads and Game Center rankings need internet.

Before you download

Is it free? Are there ads?

Yes, it is free to download and play, with no in-app purchases or paid campaign unlocks. Google rewarded ads are optional: choose an available ad for one weapon deployment. Ordinary gameplay never requires an ad. You do not need an Apple Arcade membership.

Can I play on my iPhone without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Download the app first, then play the single-player campaign and keep local records offline. Optional rewarded ads and worldwide Game Center rankings require internet. See the offline play guide for setup and limits.

How was the game made?

Generative AI was used extensively in development and promotion, including code, artwork, audio and promotional video. Gameplay recordings show the game; illustrated promotional scenes are not gameplay.

What kind of game is it?

It is a single-player action arcade game about timing, patience, and tapping flies. Your iPhone’s sensors make the flies react to movement and your approach. There are 51 levels, combo rewards, and three-star challenges. Learn how to play.

Which devices can play it?

Shut Up Fly runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later and iPad with iPadOS 18 or later. Check your software version in Settings, General, About, and use the App Store listing to confirm compatibility before downloading.

Why does it ask for camera and motion access?

These signals power Fly Sense on your device. Camera images are not recorded or saved, and raw sensor readings are not sent to the developer. The game does not use the microphone. Optional ads have separate data practices. Read the privacy policy or get permission setup help.
