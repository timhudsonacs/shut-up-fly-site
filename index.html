Before you download

Is Shut Up Fly really free, with no ads?

Yes. Shut Up Fly is free to download and play, with no ads, in-app purchases, or paid campaign unlocks. You do not need an Apple Arcade membership.

Can I play on my iPhone without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Download the app first, then play the single-player campaign and keep local records offline. Optional worldwide Game Center rankings require internet. See the offline play guide for setup and limits.

What kind of game is it?

It is a single-player action arcade game about timing, patience, and tapping flies. Your iPhone’s sensors make the flies react to movement and sound. There are 51 levels, combo rewards, and three-star challenges. Learn how to play.

Which iPhones can play it?

Shut Up Fly requires iOS 18 or later on iPhone. Check your iOS version in Settings, General, About, and use the App Store listing to confirm compatibility before downloading.

Why does it ask for camera, microphone, and motion access?

These signals power Fly Sense on your device. Camera images and microphone audio are not recorded or saved, and raw sensor readings are not sent to the developer. Read the privacy policy or get permission setup help.
