SHUT UP FLY — PRESS FACTS
Updated October 7, 2026 for public version 1.12.
Release status: public US and GB lookup returned 1.12 on October 7, 2026. Version 1.13 (58) is awaiting review and is not represented by this screenshot set.

Developer: Tim Hudson / THUD Studio
Platform: iPhone, iOS 18 or later
Price: Free, optional rewarded ads, no in-app purchases
Genre: Single-player action arcade with Flyduko logic puzzles
Campaign: 51 levels; ordinary campaign rounds last 30 seconds
Difficulty: Regular, Medium and Neon, with separate progress
Collection: 13 weapons and rewards, including Fly of Valour
Puzzles: 300 Flyduko puzzles; one fly per row, column and region, none touching even diagonally
Offline: Campaign and local records after download
Online: Optional rewarded ads and Game Center rankings

The Armoury banks rewards from accurate four-hit fly/maggot recipes. Opening it pauses the hunt. Each deployment uses its own weapon rules: for example, three Salt Shotgun shots or unlimited Newspaper swats for the level.
Flyduko newly solved puzzles earn prize-machine weapons to bring back to the hunt. Replaying a previously rewarded puzzle does not earn another prize.
Watch / Win Weapons is optional. One completed eligible rewarded ad earns one deployment. The 1.12 Supply Shack holds up to 99 ad-earned deployments of each of 11 eligible weapons. This is a stock cap, not a daily allowance. Frog and Fly of Valour are excluded from ad choices. Ad availability varies.

Version 1.12 update: refreshed Supply Shack, clearer island mission and Flyduko weapon-return feedback, smoother celebrations, more responsive first Newspaper and Electric Racquet strikes. The 51 levels and 300 puzzles are existing features, not all new in this update.

Camera and motion power local Fly Sense reactions. Camera images are not recorded, saved or sent to the developer. No microphone is used. Optional ad services process data; consult the full privacy policy.
