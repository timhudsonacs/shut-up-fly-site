SHORT DESCRIPTION
An island full of flies. An Armoury full of bad ideas. Shut Up Fly turns your
iPhone into a tactile fly-squishing arcade. Clear flies and maggots against a
30-second campaign clock, earn weapons with four-hit combos, and restore
the island across 51 levels.

FEATURES
- Fly Sense responds to live on-device movement and approach signals.
- Clear every fly and maggot before the campaign timer expires.
- Four-hit combos earn weapon rewards.
- 51 campaign levels and island regions to restore.
- Separate Regular, Medium, and Neon progression and Game Center boards.
- Three-star results, local records, and Hall of Fame.
- Flyduko puzzles award weapons for newly solved puzzles.
- Optional rewarded ads earn a selected weapon deployment.
- Single-player campaign and local records work offline after download.
- Ads and optional worldwide Apple Game Center rankings require internet.
- No developer account or in-app purchases.

PRIVACY
Camera and motion inputs are processed on the device for fly reactions.
Camera images are not recorded or saved. The game does not use the microphone.
Optional Google AdMob ads involve advertising and diagnostic data processing.
Details: https://shutupfly.timhudson.com/privacy/
