SHORT DESCRIPTION
Shut Up Fly turns your iPhone into a fly-swatting arcade. Stay still, let a fly
land, then tap before it escapes. Real-world movement and sudden sound changes
affect the hunt. Stop maggots hatching, complete four-hit combo keys, and earn
weapons including La Chancla across 51 increasingly difficult environments.

FEATURES
- Fly Sense responds to live on-device sensor signals.
- A run ends if 12 live flies take over.
- Four-hit combos unlock weapon rewards.
- 51 environments, three-star mastery, local records, and Hall of Fame.
- Single-player campaign and local records work offline after download.
- Optional worldwide Apple Game Center rankings require internet.
- No ads or in-app purchases.

PRIVACY
Camera, microphone, and motion signals are processed on the device.
Camera images and audio are not recorded or saved.
Details: https://shutupfly.timhudson.com/privacy/
