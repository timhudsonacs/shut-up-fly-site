01 / Release scope

Which version are you reviewing?

Public App Store version: 1.15. Released October 16, 2026; verified through Apple’s US and UK listings on October 16.

What changed in 1.15

A quicker introduction, clearer 30-second level guidance and a final-countdown stopwatch, new maggot splats, livelier Flyduko prize reveals, improved weapon guidance, and unused weapons carrying into the next game. Sound, animations, sharing, and celebration pauses also received fixes.

The press kit’s six screenshots show version 1.12 with staged progress and inventory. The launch promo includes earlier interface screens. Neither is presented as a version 1.15 capture. This guide summarizes published game information; it is not a fresh device playtest.

02 / The game

Pack the flies. Prepare their island.

  1. Launch and follow the introduction, difficulty choice, and training prompts if shown. No developer login or purchase is required.
  2. Continue from the island into the next available campaign level. Hold the phone steady, watch Fly Sense, and tap landed flies. Crush maggots before they hatch.
  3. Clear every fly and maggot before the 30-second campaign timer expires. Training has its own guided flow.
  4. Complete four-hit fly/maggot sequences to earn weapons. Open the Armoury in a campaign round to inspect or deploy inventory.
  5. Finish levels to restore island regions for the flies, who get the island in the end. Every packed fly is being sent on an expensive worldwide vacation. Progress is separate for Regular, Medium, and Neon. Completing the island on a difficulty unlocks the Fly Break finale.

The single-player campaign and local records work offline after download. Optional Game Center rankings require internet and Apple sign-in; cancelling authentication does not block ordinary gameplay.

03 / Optional rewards

Weapons are earned. Ads are a choice.

Junkyard Picture Show

Choose an available optional weapon offer in the Junkyard Picture Show. Each completed rewarded ad earns one use of the displayed weapon. Bank up to 99 ad-earned deployments per eligible weapon. This is a stock cap, not a daily allowance. Follow the offer and inventory shown in your installed version.

Ads need internet and availability varies. Leaving before the ad provider awards the reward does not grant a weapon. Ordinary play remains available without watching ads; there are no in-app purchases.

Flyduko

Take on 300 fly-themed logic puzzles and win prize-machine weapons to bring back to the hunt. Gameplay combo rewards are another way to earn weapons.

04 / Permissions & privacy

On-device reactions.
No microphone.

Camera frames and motion-related signals power Fly Sense locally. Camera images are not recorded, saved, or sent to the developer. The game does not use the microphone. Denying a sensor permission reduces fly-reaction inputs; ordinary play remains available.

Progress, inventory, settings, and local records are stored on the iPhone. Optional rankings use Apple Game Center.

Optional ads use Google AdMob and its consent service. The app requests non-personalized ads and disables Google’s publisher first-party identifier. This does not mean no data is processed: the SDK may process device identifiers, IP-derived general location, ad interactions, and diagnostics. Gameplay camera images and raw sensor inputs are not supplied to Google.

Available ad privacy choices can be revisited from Privacy or the reward offer. Declining consent does not prevent ordinary gameplay, although it may affect ad availability.
