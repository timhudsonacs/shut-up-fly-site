01 / Release scope

Which version are you reviewing?

Public App Store version: 1.11. Verified October 4, 2026. It includes the 51-level campaign, island restoration, training, Flyduko, optional rewarded ads, and Game Center rankings.

Update in preparation

The new Supply Shack reward cards, independent inventory caps of 99 ad-earned deployments per eligible weapon, island mission card, Flyduko earned-weapon announcement, and latest animation improvements are prepared for a future update. 02 / The game

Win back the island.

  1. Launch and choose LET’S GO. Follow the first-play difficulty choice and training prompts if shown. No developer login or purchase is required.
  2. Continue from the island into the next available campaign level. Hold the phone steady, watch Fly Sense, and tap landed flies. Crush maggots before they hatch.
  3. Clear every fly and maggot before the 30-second campaign timer expires. Training has its own guided flow.
  4. Complete four-hit fly/maggot sequences to earn weapons. Open the Armoury in a campaign round to inspect or deploy inventory.
  5. Finish levels to restore island regions. Progress is separate for Regular, Medium, and Neon. Completing the island on a difficulty unlocks the Fly Break finale.

The single-player campaign and local records work offline after download. Optional Game Center rankings require internet and Apple sign-in; cancelling authentication does not block ordinary gameplay.

03 / Optional rewards

Weapons are earned. Ads are a choice.

Watch / Win Weapons

  1. Start an ordinary campaign round after training, open Armoury, choose an eligible weapon, and open Watch / Win Weapons.
  2. Review the displayed weapon offer. Complete any required Google privacy flow, then choose the watch-ad action when an ad is available.
  3. A reward is granted only after Google’s earned-reward callback: one deployment of the selected weapon. Exiting before that callback earns none. Ad availability varies and requires internet.
  4. Return to the game to use the saved weapon. Ordinary play remains available without ads.
Upcoming update: independent stock limits

The Supply Shack shows the selected weapon count and total ad-earned haul. Each of the 11 eligible weapons can store up to 99 ad-earned deployments. This is a stock limit, not a daily allowance. Spending one reopens a slot. Gameplay and Flyduko rewards are separate. Earlier builds have different limits; use their on-screen offer.

Ads are not offered in training, the practice Range, or daily challenges. Frog and Fly of Valour are gameplay rewards, not ad choices. Rounds using ad-earned weapons are excluded from ranked score eligibility.

Flyduko

Open Flyduko from the game’s navigation. Each newly solved puzzle awards one prize-machine weapon. Replaying an already rewarded puzzle does not earn another. Saved Spins retrieves an earned, unclaimed spin.

Upcoming update: returning to Shut Up Fly from Flyduko gameplay or its reward machine shows newly earned weapons before returning to the game. Dismissing that announcement does not grant a second reward.

04 / Permissions & privacy

On-device reactions.
No microphone.

Camera frames and motion-related signals power Fly Sense locally. Camera images are not recorded, saved, or sent to the developer. The game does not use the microphone. Denying a sensor permission reduces fly-reaction inputs; ordinary play remains available.

Progress, inventory, settings, and local records are stored on the iPhone. Optional rankings use Apple Game Center.

Optional ads use Google AdMob and its consent service. The app requests non-personalized ads and disables Google’s publisher first-party identifier. This does not mean no data is processed: the SDK may process device identifiers, IP-derived general location, ad interactions, and diagnostics. Gameplay camera images and raw sensor inputs are not supplied to Google.

Available ad privacy choices can be revisited from Privacy or the reward offer. Declining consent does not prevent ordinary gameplay, although it may affect ad availability.
