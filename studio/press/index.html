01 / The studio

Made with character.

MUNTED Studio is Tim Hudson’s independent iOS game studio. Its games bring expressive artwork, memorable characters and satisfying things to do with your hands to iPhone and iPad: a ridiculous fly hunt in Shut Up Fly, a quiet counterfeit-cash puzzle in Bill Sergeant, and a ride down the mountain in Whoopdy Doo.

02 / Three ways to play

Pick your kind of mischief.

Shut Up Fly

Hold steady.
Let it land. Squish.

Clear flies and maggots in 30-second arcade rounds, restore an island across 51 levels, and earn an outrageous Armoury. Flyduko adds 300 logic puzzles whose weapon prizes return to the hunt.

Free · iOS/iPadOS 18+
Optional rewarded ads. No in-app purchases.

Get Shut Up Fly ↗

Gameplay, screenshots & facts →

Bill Sergeant

Good money.
Bad company.

Look at both sides of a bill. Put genuine notes in the money bag and counterfeits in the trash. A relaxed currency puzzle with a meticulous supervisor, a dry sense of humour and no ticking clock.

Free · iOS/iPadOS 17+
Illustrated currency puzzle.

Whoopdy Doo

A little mountain.
A lot of trouble.

Pump through rollers, pick a jump line and find your balance on the way down. Ride three trails, chase medals, or discover the mountain’s hidden places in Free Ride.

Free · iOS/iPadOS 17+
Landscape play. No account required.
